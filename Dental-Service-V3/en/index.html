Treatments

Root canal (Endodontics)

Treatment of the inside of the tooth when the pulp is affected by deep decay, trauma or infection, aiming to remove the pain and keep the tooth in place.

  • Clinical and imaging assessment before it is indicated
  • Local anaesthesia and isolation of the tooth
  • May require more than one session, depending on the case

Aesthetic restoration

Rebuilding chipped, worn or decayed teeth with materials that reproduce the colour and gloss of natural enamel.

  • Shade selection together with you
  • Finishing and polishing on the same day in most cases
  • Suitable for front and back teeth

Cleaning and prophylaxis

Removal of plaque and tartar, with personalised hygiene guidance. This is the maintenance procedure that sustains the result of any treatment.

  • Recommended every six months, on average
  • Includes brushing and flossing instruction
  • The starting point for diagnosing other problems

Teeth whitening

Whitening of tooth colour under a supervised protocol, indicated after an assessment that rules out decay, sensitivity and other conditions that need treating first.

  • Prior assessment is required
  • Can be done in the surgery or with a home tray
  • Temporary sensitivity is possible and is monitored by the clinic
